MARINA HEAT

An open-world crime game in Marina Verde — sunset city, stolen cars, five stars of trouble.

Steal cars, run nine story jobs, race the streets, shop for gear, and lose the Marina PD. The heli comes out at three stars.

WASD / Arrows — drive or walk  •  E — enter / exit car, talk, shop, save
Space — handbrake drift  •  Shift — nitro  •  F / Click — fire pistol
1 / 2 — fists / pistol  •  R — radio station  •  Esc — pause
Follow the ★ on the minimap for jobs, ◆ for races, fares and deliveries. The safehouse (H) saves and clears wanted.

PAUSED

Marina Verde waits for no one. Well — except now.

BUSTED

The Marina PD caught up with you. Fine paid, car impounded, and you wake up at the safehouse.
